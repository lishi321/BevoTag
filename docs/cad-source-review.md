# CAD model source review

Reviewed **2026-10-21**. This note records additional evidence used to improve the room-and-wall model and identifies what it cannot establish. The app renders source-derived meshes plus a separate inferred EER/ECJ enclosure layer, and exports structured IFC4 wall solids, room footprints and labeled enclosure proxies. It is not a native architectural authoring model or certified as-built CAD. See [floor height sources](floor-height-sources.md) for the ECJ elevation table and its normalization.

## ECJ

- **Record elevations recovered:** [Sarlo's Virginia Tech dissertation](https://hdl.handle.net/10919/84477), [PDF Appendix C](https://vtechworks.lib.vt.edu/bitstreams/b9653165-72de-4e1e-9470-21996593bbe9/download#page=189), pages 189-191 (printed 171-173), reproduces WJE 2013 sheets S3.1/S3.2 and a sensor coordinate table. This supports 11 historical level elevations after subtracting Level 1 EL 552 ft. Level 6 is an estimate because its elevation tag conflicts with the dimension chain. SB, 11M and the multi-surface Roof (TBD) plan remain unresolved.
- **Horizontal corroboration:** the record sheets show structural-axis spans of 134 ft and 102 ft, including central 22 ft and 26 ft bays. These corroborate plan scale; they are not exterior-face measurements or justification for indiscriminate rescaling.
- **Level 4 extraction correction:** the local `ECJ.pdf` page 6 is stored with 270-degree page rotation. Its displayed sheet and `to_frame` registration use rotated coordinates, whereas raw PDF drawing primitives do not. Applying the PDF page rotation before clipping and projection corrects the coordinate mismatch. This is a source-coordinate correction, not a guessed building rotation or field measurement. See the building's existing `VERIFICATION.md` and the architecture extractor.
- **Separate roof surfaces:** the [official roof RFQ](https://construction.utexas.edu/26dbl001-cp2001733-ecj-roof-replacement-rfq) distinguishes the Level 11 lower roof/mechanical-penthouse floor and upper penthouse roof. Their different heights must not be flattened into one apparently documented roof plane.
- **Penthouse enclosure:** the WJE record elevations reproduced on dissertation PDF pages 189–190 show penthouse roof EL 711 ft. Subtracting Level 1 EL 552 ft gives a **+159 ft enclosure top**. The coarse shell follows the inner level 11 penthouse perimeter on local plan page 13; outer nested roof/parapet projections are excluded from its context walls. This sourced top does not resolve the composite R sheet's elevation, roof buildup, parapets or survey datum. Page 14's 11M is internal and page 15's R is a composite reference, so neither adds an exterior storey.
- **Removable roof surfaces:** the lower roof follows historical EL 694 minus EL 552 = **+142 ft**; the penthouse roof follows **+159 ft**. Separate inferred caps use reviewed local roof outlines and extend 0.5 ft downward for display. That thickness is not a measured roof assembly, and the caps do not assign an elevation to the composite R sheet.
- **Exterior footprint review:** local plan pages 1–15 supply the broad lower podium, repeated tower and recessed penthouse outlines recorded in `Experiments/Campus_Map/collector/buildings/ECJ/exterior-review.json`. These are coarse plan traces with simplified entrance/column details, not surveyed facade coordinates. Original room/wall arrays are preserved; mode-specific context is a separate derived layer.
- **Plan currency:** the examined upper-level and roof sheets bear a May 5, 2022 title-block date. [TDLR TABS2026014484](https://www.tdlr.texas.gov/TABS/Search/Print/TABS2026014484), registered March 9, 2026, describes complete renovation of Levels 7-8 and partial scope on 9-11. [BGK's project description](https://www.bgkarchitects.com/project/ecj-engineering-renovation-at-austin-adaptive) corroborates the laboratory renovation. These sources identify a currency risk; replacement partition drawings were not recovered and their changes are not claimed as applied.

## EER

- **Atrium geometry:** [AISC's 2019 IDEAS2 Awards](https://www.aisc.org/contentassets/e18e7275c8ac4615af25d3f92fa6ec40/ideas2_promo_9x9_2019_web.pdf#page=30), PDF page 30 (printed page 28), describes a 150 by 70 ft skylight, west entrance at Level 3 and west bridges on Levels 5-8. [UT's opening account](https://magazine.engr.utexas.edu/2017/a-unifying-force) independently describes the 70 ft atrium width.
- **Two distinct overhead structures:** [UT's account](https://magazine.engr.utexas.edu/2017/a-unifying-force) describes viewing the high shading canopy through the angled atrium skylight; [Ennead](https://ennead.com/work/eerc/) identifies a glass-roofed three-story atrium between the towers. The lower glazed roof must not be confused with the separate upper open canopy. The removable skylight is a flat approximation at the schematic top of model level 3, with uncertain exact elevation and slope; all EER vertical values remain assumed. Tower/connector roof caps remain separate, and the upper shading canopy is unmodeled.
- **Model implication:** the central atrium must be reviewed as a void crossed by real bridges, not an enclosed room on every floor. Skylight dimensions alone do not locate every slab opening or set a bridge outline. Review the local plan's actual linework and labels, preserve bridges and floor surfaces with evidence, and retain a reproducible record of any exclusions. This note does not claim every atrium opening is completely verified or prescribe final polygon counts.
- **Vertical limits:** the [architect's section](https://www.archdaily.com/881939/cockrell-school-of-engineering-education-research-center-eerc-ennead-architects/59e83b27b22e38b6a3000639-cockrell-school-of-engineering-education-research-center-eerc-ennead-architects-section) shows unequal spacing but supplies no usable numerical floor schedule. A 70 ft atrium width and 150 ft skylight span cannot determine floor heights.
- **Additional design evidence:** the [architect-authored project account](https://www.theplan.it/eng/architecture/engineering-education-and-research-center-en) describes a 30 ft site grade change, two towers and a three-story atrium. The [envelope consultants' coordination paper](https://iibec.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/2017-CONV-Coltzer-Ozbun.pdf), PDF pages 5–8, discusses the Revit coordination model and envelope assemblies. These corroborate building organization; neither supplies a dimensioned floor schedule or an accessible native BIM model for import.
- **Applied corrections:** local source-plan pages 4, 7 and 9 now have explicit reviewed regions in `collector/buildings/EER/geometry-review.json`, bound to the PDF SHA256. They remove false floor surfaces and extruded roof-member projections on levels 3, 6 and 8. Page 7's actual bridge boundaries restore a 394.6 ft² west-bridge footprint. The generator applies these corrections before saving/previews; IFC retains their provenance. This is a targeted review, not confirmation of every atrium or bridge boundary.
- **Exterior footprint review:** local plan pages 1–9 supply coarse enclosure traces in `Experiments/Campus_Map/collector/buildings/EER/exterior-review.json`. Major atrium openings and setbacks are retained rather than filled with a convex hull. This provides nine inferred storey enclosures, but no numerical EER level schedule, facade assembly, verified roof elevation or measured thickness. Lower-level source clipping, small recesses and terraces still require review.
- **Plan currency:** [TDLR TABS2027000248](https://www.tdlr.texas.gov/TABS/Projects/TABS2027000248), registered September 3, 2026, describes approximately 3,000 square feet changing from library to office, scheduled September 8, 2026-January 25, 2027. The record supplies no floor/room IDs or replacement layout. The schedule is not proof of construction progress or completion. No speculative office partitions have been added from this notice.

## ASE

- [UT Aerospace facilities](https://ae.utexas.edu/about/facilities/) identifies the building's 2002 opening and comprehensive 2019 renovation. This establishes history, not an elevation schedule or current wall coordinates.
- [TDLR TABS2025013611](https://www.tdlr.texas.gov/TABS/Search/Project/TABS2025013611), registered March 7, 2025, describes a 1,000 square foot classroom split involving new walls and doors. Scheduled dates are April 1, 2025-March 1, 2026; the record reports Project Closed. No floor/room ID or as-built layout is provided. Administrative closure does not establish the installed partition coordinates, and the model does not invent that change.
- Existing NOA/ASE plan names share the same building identity. Preserve source-specific plan evidence and require current dimensioned drawings for any further room or vertical changes.

## Evidence handling and remaining work

`building.json` carries `geometry_review` with review date, concise limitations and primary sources. It complements elevation provenance and extraction metadata; it does not certify every polygon. PDF `ModDate` values are file metadata, not survey or as-built dates.

The separate EER/ECJ exterior reviews are bound to source PDF hashes and source pages and are applied by `exterior_review.py` after room/wall review. They produce **22 inferred storey enclosures** using a **0.5 ft assumed inward shell**. Bands span exterior levels or an explicitly identified top; a final band without top evidence uses the editable display wall height. Doors and windows are closed in these shells without assigning opening positions, materials or RF properties. Original room/wall arrays remain unchanged, and disabling Continuous exterior restores their source view/export. Default IFC exports distinguish inferred enclosures as `IfcBuildingElementProxy` and retain all space reference curves. A continuous appearance does not establish a completed facade, roof assembly or accurate as-built volume.

Remaining work includes current renovation plans, confirmed door/window openings, slab thicknesses, actual wall heights and materials, ECJ's unresolved levels/roof surfaces, and EER/ASE floor elevations. Extracted labels, room connectivity and small wall fragments still need comparison with drawings and field measurements. Position-estimation accuracy must be validated separately against measured ground-truth locations; better geometry alone is not an accuracy result.

Removable roof, terrace and skylight solids are separate inferred objects, never replacements for the original room/wall arrays. Their 0.5 ft display thickness extends below the indicated surface. UI and export toggles can omit them independently of exterior enclosures; IFC retains separate proxy objects and evidence. Flat caps do not model actual drainage slopes, roof build-ups, parapets or glazing assemblies.
