Verdict and how to use this doc

The revised Project Definition would most likely earn Complete: Meets Basic Expectations today. The formatting and structure problems from the Writing TA feedback are mostly fixed. What keeps it from Exceeds is content: missing specifications, a few citations that may not support their claims, and places where the doc commits to a specific design.

This review covers docs/project-definition/main.tex as pushed to main (commit 9c0a6c9). It checks the draft against Project Definition rubric.pdf, the assignment description, and the ECE Style Guide. The ratings below are an estimate, not a TA's grade.

Issues fall into three groups:

  • Needs team discussion: decisions or numbers the group has to agree on, ideally at the next meeting. Each comes with a question and a suggested answer.
  • Needs attention: real fixes that one person can make without a group decision. Assign an owner.
  • Trivial fixes: a few minutes each, for anyone.

Use the Status column to track progress, and comment on any row you disagree with.

Meeting agenda

A 40-minute meeting covers the 11 decisions. The other 19 fixes only need an owner and a deadline, not discussion.

  1. Verdict and scorecard (3 min): agree on the target of reaching Exceeds.
